Passive Revocation Architecture

Smallstep eliminates heavy Certificate Revocation Lists (CRLs) and fragile OCSP stapling by issuing ephemeral 24-hour certificates renewed automatically via background daemon.

⚡ Passive Revocation (Modern Zero-Trust)

Certificates naturally expire in 24 hours. Compromised certificates become useless within hours without network roundtrips.

Issued (T=0) Auto-Renew (T=16h) Expire (T=24h)
  • No single point of failure (no CRL/OCSP responders)
  • Privacy-preserving: CA doesn't see TLS handshakes
  • Automated mTLS renewal via step ca renew
⚠️ Active Revocation (Legacy PKI)

Certificates live for 1 to 5 years. Checking revocation requires querying public CRL downloads (megabytes) or OCSP servers on every connection.

Issued (Year 0) Stale CRL Cache Expire (Year 1+)
  • High latency on TLS handshake
  • Fails open if OCSP responder is down (insecure)
  • Fails closed if OCSP responder times out (outage)
